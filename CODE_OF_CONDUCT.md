# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Strongfy is committed to a respectful, professional, inclusive, and harassment-free community. Contributors and participants must communicate constructively, respect different perspectives, accept feedback in good faith, and focus discussions on the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, and any conduct that would reasonably make another person feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Scope

This policy applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, release notes, and other public or private communication made on behalf of Strongfy.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, commits, code, issues, or other material that violates this policy. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when appropriate.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Include relevant links, context, and supporting evidence when possible. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
